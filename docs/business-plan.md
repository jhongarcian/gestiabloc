# Gestiabloc Business Plan

## Product

Gestiabloc is a multi-tenant SaaS CRM for agencies and service-driven businesses. The product is focused on operational management: contacts, tasks, scheduling, follow-ups, collaboration, reporting, and AI-assisted workflows.

## Target Customers

- Small businesses that need a simple operational CRM
- Growing agencies that manage multiple workflows and staff
- Operations-heavy teams that need scheduling, collaboration, and reporting
- Larger organizations that require stronger permissions, analytics, and process visibility

## Value Proposition

- Centralize daily operations in one workspace
- Reduce manual follow-up and coordination work
- Give teams shared visibility across tasks, calendars, comments, and client activity
- Use AI features to speed up operational work such as summaries, recommendations, and workflow assistance
- Scale seat capacity and operational controls as the customer grows

## Pricing Strategy

The app uses three subscription tiers with increasing seat capacity, AI usage, storage, and operational controls.

### Basic

- Price: $29/month
- Seats: up to 3 team members
- Positioning: entry plan for small businesses, startups, and small teams managing daily operations
- Includes:
  - Task management
  - Calendar and scheduling
  - Real-time notifications
  - Team assignments
  - Internal comments
  - File attachments
  - Basic dashboard and reporting
  - Mobile-friendly interface
  - AI assistant features
- AI allowance: 100 AI actions per month
- Storage: 5 GB
- Support: email support

### Pro

- Price: $59/month
- Seats: up to 10 team members
- Positioning: growth plan for agencies and operations teams with multiple workflows and staff
- Includes everything in Basic, plus:
  - Advanced task management
  - Team and department organization
  - Advanced scheduling and calendar management
  - Live updates and collaboration
  - Activity history and audit tracking
  - Custom task categories and tags
  - Advanced dashboard and reporting
  - Exportable reports
  - Expanded AI operational assistant features
- AI allowance: 750 AI actions per month
- Storage: 25 GB
- Support: priority email support

### Business

- Price: $119/month
- Seats: up to 25 team members
- Positioning: advanced plan for larger organizations and operations-focused businesses
- Includes everything in Pro, plus:
  - Multi-team and multi-department management
  - Advanced permissions and role management
  - Advanced operational dashboards
  - Detailed activity and audit logs
  - Advanced analytics and reporting
  - API access
  - Webhook support
  - Priority real-time infrastructure
  - Dedicated onboarding assistance
  - Advanced AI operational tools
  - Custom workflow support
- AI allowance: 3,000 AI actions per month
- Storage: 100 GB
- Support: priority support and dedicated onboarding assistance

## Go-To-Market Direction

- Use the public homepage as the top-of-funnel entry point
- Push most users toward Pro as the default recommended plan
- Let customers self-select by team size and operational complexity
- Convert signups through a simple trial-first registration flow
- Add checkout and billing automation next so selected plans become paid subscriptions immediately after onboarding

## Expansion Opportunities

- Stripe subscription checkout and billing portal
- AI usage metering and overage billing
- Seat-based upsells
- Annual billing discounts
- Enterprise onboarding and support packages
- API and automation-focused add-ons
